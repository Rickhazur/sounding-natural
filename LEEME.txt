LOS 10 CHUNKS (validados pedagógicamente, 2026-10-15 —
reporte en research_notes/m1l2-meetings-validation-20261015/):

🟢 OPEN — "start the meeting"
1. Let's get started.          "Let's get started. Good morning, everyone."      [spoken]
2. First on the agenda,        "First on the agenda, the sales numbers."        [spoken]

🟢 JOIN IN — "enter the conversation" (🧭 portable: fires ANYWHERE)
3. Can I jump in here?         "Can I jump in here?"                            [spoken — ask to ADD]
4. Sorry to interrupt, but…    "Sorry to interrupt, but this is urgent."        [spoken — apologize, CUT IN]

🟡 CHECK ALIGNMENT — "the judgment call" (🧭 portable)
5. Any questions so far?       "Any questions so far?"                          [spoken — checks COMPREHENSION]
6. Are we aligned on this?     "Are we aligned on this?"                        [spoken — checks AGREEMENT]

🟢 STEER — "direct the flow"
7. Moving on to…               "Moving on to the budget."                       [spoken]
8. Let's circle back to that.  "Let's circle back to that after lunch."        [spoken — promise the return]

🟢 CLOSE — "land with actions"
9. To wrap up,                 "To wrap up, great progress today."              [spoken]
10. So, action items:          "So, action items: Ana sends the report by Friday."  [spoken — owner + deadline]

CLAVES PEDAGÓGICAS DE LA LECCIÓN:
- #5 ≠ #6: "comprehension ≠ agreement" — verifica que ENTENDIERON (#5)
  antes de verificar que ESTÁN DE ACUERDO (#6). Nunca "Does that
  make sense?" (el 54% de profesionales lo percibe condescendiente).
- #3 ≠ #4: dos TONOS, no sinónimos — #3 pide permiso para AGREGAR,
  #4 se disculpa por CORTAR. El camino medio entre el silencio
  (trampa del hispanohablante) y la interrupción brusca.
- Cierre con acción: secuencia "To wrap up," + "So, action items:"
  y la regla no escrita: cada item lleva DUEÑO + FECHA, o se evapora.
