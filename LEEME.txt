LOS 10 CHUNKS (validados pedagógicamente — reporte:
workspace/research_notes/m1l1-revalidation-20261015/report.md.
Veredicto: 10 KEEP, 0 REPLACE):

🔀 OPEN & SHIFT (cambiar de tema)
1. By the way,            "By the way, the client call was moved to Thursday."          [spoken]
2. Speaking of which,     "Speaking of which, the budget was approved this morning."   [spoken]
3. That reminds me,       "That reminds me, I need to send you the revised timeline." [spoken]

🔍 CLARIFY (aclarar / reparar)
4. In other words,        "In other words, we deliver the first phase by Friday."      [both]
5. As a matter of fact,   "As a matter of fact, we have already started the rollout."  [spoken]
6. As I was saying,       "As I was saying, the proposal needs one more review."      [spoken — repair/resumption move]

⚖️ WEIGH IN (posicionarse antes de opinar)
7. On the other hand,     "On the other hand, the risks are real and worth weighing."  [both — 🔁 spiral]
8. To be honest,          "To be honest, I think the deadline is too tight."           [spoken — 🔁 spiral]
9. For what it's worth,   "For what it's worth, the competitor launched a new product."[spoken — 🔁 spiral]

🏁 CLOSE (cerrar)
10. At the end of the day, "At the end of the day, the client decides."                [spoken — ONE per conversation]

PEDAGOGÍA DE LA LECCIÓN (está dentro de las tarjetas):
· Función primero, traducción después: cada tarjeta dice QUÉ HACE
  (shift / reformulate / hedge / close) antes de la glosa.
· Frequency = safety: sin estos marcadores, el inglés suena
  telegráfico — la ubicuidad es la licencia del registro.
· El clúster weigh-in (7/8/9) es la habilidad central + nota
  🔁 SPIRAL-FORWARD en cada tarjeta: "L1 builds the positioning
  reflex — Module 3's opinion chunks supply the payloads."
· #10 lleva nota de cliché: uno por conversación, nunca apilados.
· #6 etiquetado como repair/resumption move.
