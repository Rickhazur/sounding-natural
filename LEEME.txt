LOS 10 PATRONES (orden pedagógico validado)
--------------------------------------------
1. First conditional — If we sign today, we'll save 10%.
2. Unless / as long as / provided that — Unless we hear otherwise, we'll proceed.
3. In case vs. if — Take the contract in case they ask for it.
4. Second conditional — If I were you, I'd renegotiate the terms.
5. What if / Supposing — What if the client pulls out at the last minute?
6. Even if — Even if they agree, we still need legal review.
7. Third conditional — If we had known, we'd have acted sooner.
8. Mixed conditional — If we had signed earlier, we'd be saving money now.
9. Wish / If only + past perfect — I wish we had hired sooner.
10. Otherwise — We have to launch in June; otherwise, we'll lose the slot.
