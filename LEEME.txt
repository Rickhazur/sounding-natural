EL NUEVO SISTEMA (10 patrones de ÉNFASIS Y FOCO, validados
pedagógicamente — las inversiones ahora viven en la Lección 3):
1. Emphatic do            "We do value your partnership."
2. It-cleft               "It was Maria who closed the deal."
3. Contrastive focus      "It's not the price, it's the timeline."
4. What-cleft             "What we need is a decision by Friday."
5. Reversed what-cleft    "A decision by Friday is what we need."
6. All-cleft              "All we need is a decision."
7. The reason… is…        "The reason I'm calling is to confirm the dates."
8. "The thing is…"        "The thing is, we need more time."
9. Object fronting        "This clause we cannot accept."
10. Complement fronting   "Critical to the deal is the timeline."
