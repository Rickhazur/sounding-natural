LOS 10 PATRONES (orden pedagógico validado)
--------------------------------------------
1. Never + inversion — Never have I seen such chaos in a negotiation.
2. Seldom / Rarely + inversion — Rarely have we witnessed such growth in a single quarter.
3. Not only... but also — Not only did she win the contract, but she doubled the revenue.
4. Under no circumstances — Under no circumstances should client data leave the office.
5. Nowhere + inversion — Nowhere in the report is the delay explained.
6. Hardly / Scarcely... when — Hardly had we arrived when the call started.
7. Only + time + inversion — Only then did I understand the risk.
8. Not until + inversion — Not until the audit ended did we relax.
9. So / Such + inversion — So compelling were the results that the board approved immediately.
10. Should / Were / Had — Should you need anything, call me.
