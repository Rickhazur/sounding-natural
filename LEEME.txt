LOS 10 PATRONES (orden pedagógico validado)
--------------------------------------------
1. Must be / can't be — She must be negotiating right now.
2. Might / may + base — The delay might cost us the contract.
3. Should be (expectation) — The report should be ready by now.
4. Must have + participle — They must have signed already.
5. Can't have + participle — He can't have missed the deadline.
6. Might / may have + participle — The client might have changed their mind.
7. Will have + participle — They'll have finished the review by now.
8. Could have + participle — We could have saved millions.
9. Should have + participle — We should have called legal first.
10. Needn't have vs. didn't need to — You needn't have printed everything.
