LOS 10 CHUNKS (validados pedagógicamente, en orden narrativo):
🎬 SET THE SCENE:
 1. Let me set the scene:        "Let me set the scene: it's 2019, and we're losing clients."   [⚖️ Both]
 2. A little background first:    "A little background first: we had just merged two teams."     [⚖️ Both]
📖 BUILD THE STORY:
 3. So there we were,             "So there we were, in a room with the board."                  [🗣️ Spoken]
 4. It all started when…          "It all started when the system crashed on launch day."         [⚖️ Both]
 5. And that's when everything changed.  (igual)                                                  [⚖️ Both]
 6. And then, out of nowhere,     "And then, out of nowhere, our biggest client called."         [🗣️ Spoken]
 7. To cut a long story short,    "To cut a long story short, we shipped in three weeks."        [🗣️ Spoken]
🎯 LAND THE POINT:
 8. In the end,                   "In the end, we doubled our revenue."                           [⚖️ Both]
 9. Looking back,                 "Looking back, the crisis was the best thing that happened."    [⚖️ Both]
10. The moral of the story?       "The moral of the story? Never skip the backup plan."          [🗣️ Spoken — NUNCA en reportes formales]

PUNTO CLAVE DE LA LECCIÓN: las historias en inglés usan DOS tiempos —
el pasado continuo pone el fondo (was/were + -ing), el pasado simple
mueve la acción. Ataca el "and then… and then…" plano.
