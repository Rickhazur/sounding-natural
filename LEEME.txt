LOS 10 PATRONES (orden pedagógico validado)
--------------------------------------------
1. Agentless passive — The results were published yesterday.
2. Modal passive — The documents must be signed today.
3. Personal reporting passive — The CEO is expected to announce the merger next week.
4. Passive infinitive — The proposal needs to be approved by Friday.
5. Causative have sth done — We had the contract reviewed by legal before signing.
6. Reported statements — She said she was ready. (¡ojo: say/tell!)
7. Reported requests — She told me to wait.
8. Reported questions — He asked if I needed help.
9. Reporting verbs + infinitive — They agreed to support us.
10. Reporting verbs + gerund — He admitted missing the deadline.
