🎙️ Your turn — the framing workout

Record yourself (phone voice memo is fine). Start the timer, speak, then compare with the model. Packaging is the grade.

Three missions — launch the frame while your content forms:

1

📰 Deliver bad news like a professional — 60 seconds

The client call starts now. Package the news with frames 7/8/9: The good news is / The bad news is / The problem is. Bad news lands professionally when it’s packaged — never dropped bare.

1:00
“The bad news is the budget is over.”🔊 Model — the bad news, packaged
“The problem is we don't have enough data.”🔊 Model — name the issue, no blame
“The good news is we finished ahead of schedule.”🔊 Model — the good news, same frame family
2

🔧 Rephrase a muddled message — 60 seconds

You just gave a confusing explanation and the room looks lost. Repair it live with the clarify trio: What I'm trying to say is / What I mean is / Let me put it this way: — groping, correcting, deliberate rephrase. Pick the right member.

1:00
“What I'm trying to say is the report isn't ready yet.”🔊 Model — groping, buys time
“What I mean is we should wait for the client.”🔊 Model — correcting, repairs it
“Let me put it this way: our current plan is too slow.”🔊 Model — deliberate rephrase, cleaner
3

🔭 Project next steps — 90 seconds

Close your update. Flag the point (Here's the thing:), hedge what you’re unsure of (As far as I know,), then project forward (What happens next is). End with direction — never trail off.

1:30
“Here's the thing: we need two more weeks.”🔊 Model — flag the point
“As far as I know, the contract ends in June.”🔊 Model — hedge honestly, once
“What happens next is we meet the client on Monday.”🔊 Model — project forward
