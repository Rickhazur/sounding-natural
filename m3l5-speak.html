📄 Read the full transcript

It's great to finally meet you in person. Let me set the scene: two years ago, our company was losing clients faster than we could sign them. So there we were — a team of forty people, working weekends, watching our numbers fall month after month. From my perspective, the problem was never our product. The key thing here is that we had stopped listening. We were pitching features while our clients were asking for solutions. I need to be honest with you: turning it around was painful. We lost two major accounts before we learned our lesson. But looking back, that pain taught us everything we know today. So what changed? We started asking one simple question in every meeting: what do you enjoy most about working with us? And then we shut up, and we listened. In the end, we didn't just recover — we grew forty percent in eighteen months. The moral of the story? Listen first. Pitch second. Thank you.
