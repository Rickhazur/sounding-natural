LOS 10 CHUNKS (validados pedagógicamente — listas finales aprobadas por Ricardo):

1. What does success look like in this role?
2. What are the biggest challenges facing the team right now?
3. How would you describe the team culture?
4. Is there anything about my background that concerns you?
5. How is performance measured here?
6. What do you enjoy most about working here?
7. What are the next steps in the process?
8. When could I expect to hear back?
9. Feel free to ask me anything
10. Thank you for your time today
