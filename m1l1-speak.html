🎙️ Your turn — the 90-second professional update

Record yourself (phone voice memo is fine). Then compare with the model. Flow is the grade.

Use chunks from at least 3 different moments (🔀 · 🔍 · ⚖️ · 🏁). The timer keeps you honest:

1

🔀 Open & shift → the stand-up update

The team is waiting. Give your update and jump to a new topic using 🔀 shift markers — no apology, no “I change topic now”: By the way, / Speaking of which, / That reminds me, __.

“By the way, the client call was moved to Thursday.”🔊 Model answer — tap to compare
“Speaking of which, the budget was approved this morning.”🔊 Shift #2 — link to a word just said
01:00record your update — two shifts minimum
2

🔍 Clarify & repair → the confusing point

Your proposal confused the room. Reformulate it with 🔍 clarify, then repair after a “colleague interrupts” — pick the thread back up: In other words, __ · As I was saying, __. Never trail off.

“In other words, we deliver the first phase by Friday.”🔊 Reformulate — same idea, clearer
“As I was saying, the proposal needs one more review.”🔊 Repair — resume after the interruption
01:15reformulate once, repair once
3

⚖️ Weigh in & close → your verdict

Full arc in 90 seconds: position before you state (⚖️), then close with the verdict (🏁). Say both out loud, then listen back. Model kit below — stance first, verdict last:

“To be honest, I think the deadline is too tight.”🔊 ⚖️ weigh in — stance preface
“At the end of the day, the client decides.”🔊 🏁 close — ONE per conversation
01:30weigh in, then land the verdict
