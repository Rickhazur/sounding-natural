🎙️ Your turn — the 60-second pitch

"Tell me about yourself" opens almost every interview. Record yourself (phone voice memo is fine). Then compare with the model. Sixty seconds is the grade.

Use chunks from at least 3 groups (🎤 · 🧭 · ❤️ · 🎯). The timer keeps you honest:

1

🎤 The opening block

Open canonical: profession + seniority, then anchor in the present. I'm a __ with __ years of experience in __ + Currently, I __.

“I'm a data analyst with eight years of experience in fintech.”🔊 🔊 Canonical — profession + seniority
“Currently, I lead the analytics team at a startup.”🔊 🔊 Present — the anchor
00:45who you are + what you do now
2

🧭 Structure it

Bridge the past and offer the overview: Most recently, I __ + Let me give you a quick overview.

“Most recently, I launched a dashboard used by two hundred clients.”🔊 🔊 Bridge — the freshest win
“Let me give you a quick overview of my background.”🔊 🔊 Overview — give YOU
00:45bridge + overview
3

🎯 Land the close

Hinge to THIS interview and hand off: That brings me to __ + Happy to dive deeper into any of that.

“That brings me to why I'm excited about this role.”🔊 🔊 Hinge — why you're here
“Happy to dive deeper into any of that.”🔊 🔊 Handoff — confidence
00:45hinge + handoff, 60 seconds total
