🎙️ Your turn — say your brand out loud

Recruiters read your profile, then they CALL you. Record yourself (phone voice memo is fine). Then compare with the model. Positioning is the grade.

Use chunks from at least 3 groups (🎯 · 📣 · 💡 · 🤝). The timer keeps you honest:

1

🎯 The 20-second headline

Say your headline out loud — client first, then niche: Helping __ achieve __ + I specialize in __.

“Helping startups achieve product-market fit.”🔊 🔊 Client first — outcome, not ego
“I specialize in bilingual e-learning design.”🔊 🔊 Niche — active verb, IN
00:45headline + niche, 20 seconds
2

📣 Availability block

Say where you stand: seniority + location + openness. With __ years of experience in __, Based in __, working worldwide, Open to __.

“With fifteen years of experience in corporate training.”🔊 🔊 Seniority — uncountable experience
“Based in Bogotá, working worldwide.”🔊 🔊 Roots + wings
01:00seniority, location, openness
3

💡 The value prop

Say what you do for clients — with the pain removed: I help __ do __ without __. Then close: Let’s connect.

“I help companies train teams without boring slides.”🔊 🔊 Value — name the pain removed
“Let’s connect and talk about learning design.”🔊 🔊 Close — two words
01:00value prop + close
