FINAL EXAM · ROLE-PLAY
This is not a quiz. This is the interview.
How it works. You will go through a complete 15–20 minute job interview in English: your 60-second pitch, 3 classic questions, 1 hard question, 1 salary question, your 3 closing questions — and a surprise offer call at the end. Each phase has a visible timer. Don’t pause between phases: real interviews don’t wait.

🤝 With a partner (recommended)

Your partner reads the interviewer lines out loud, runs the timers, and scores you with the 4-axis rubric at the end. They play the hiring manager in the offer call.

🎥 Solo

Record yourself on your phone. Read each interviewer line, answer out loud (never in your head), then score yourself honestly with the rubric. Honesty is the whole point.

Rules.
  • Answer everything out loud, in English, in full sentences.
  • Use the course chunks — that’s what they’re for.
  • Respect the timers. When time’s up, you stop.
  • No notes during the interview. Your preparation is the 11 lessons.

PHASE 6 OF 6 · LESSON 11 · THE TWIST
The Offer Call
The scene. The interview is over. Two days later, your phone rings. It’s the hiring manager — played by your partner, or imagined out loud if you’re solo.
📞 Hiring manager (read out loud):

“Hi [your name], this is Alex from [company]. I’m calling with great news — we’d like to offer you the position! The base salary is $X per month, plus health insurance and 15 vacation days. We’d love to have your answer by Friday. What do you think?”
You must do 3 things — out loud, in English:
  1. Thank them for the offer (never skip the courtesy).
  2. Ask for time to consider — never accept on the spot.
  3. Probe one term — the salary band, flexibility, or a benefit.
