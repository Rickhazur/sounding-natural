🎙️ Your turn — the professional email + the follow-up call

The thank-you email is a written genre — so first you master it on paper, then you deliver it out loud. Read it like a professional. Then make the follow-up call.

Read the email like a professional, then make the follow-up call. The timer keeps you honest:

1

📧 The opening block — read it like a professional

Open the email canonical: gratitude + interest. Thank you for taking the time to speak with me today + I enjoyed learning more about __. Read it OUT LOUD with professional tone — not monotone.

“Thank you for taking the time to speak with me today.”🔊 🔊 Canonical — gratitude + day
“I enjoyed learning more about your expansion plans in the region.”🔊 🔊 Gerund — enjoyed LEARNING
01:00gratitude + interest, professional tone
2

📧 The follow-up block

The nudge + the reaffirmation: I wanted to follow up on __ + Just checking in regarding __ + I'm still very interested in the role.

“I wanted to follow up on our interview last Tuesday.”🔊 🔊 Polite past — wanted
“Just checking in regarding the next steps in the process.”🔊 🔊 The real nudge
“I'm still very interested in the role.”🔊 🔊 Reaffirm — still
01:00nudge + reaffirmation
3

📞 The follow-up call — mini role-play

Now say it, don't write it. You're calling the recruiter: I'm calling to follow up on my interview last Tuesday. Then the check-in: Just checking in regarding the next steps. Natural, not read.

“I wanted to follow up on our interview last Tuesday.”🔊 🔊 Model — the nudge
“Looking forward to hearing from you.”🔊 🔊 Model — the close
00:45the call — natural, not read
