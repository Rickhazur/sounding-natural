Module 3 · Lesson 2 · Speak

🎤 Your 90 seconds

Three missions. Say each one out loud — then check yourself against the list. Recording yourself on your phone is the fastest way to improve.
MISSION 1 · 🎬 SET THE SCENE

🎬 Open the case study

You're opening a case-study presentation for new hires. First 20 seconds: set the scene so they care about what comes next.
Say it out loud: open with chunk 1 or 2, then add one line of background. Under 20 seconds.
🔊 Model answer
"Let me set the scene: it's 2019, and we're losing clients. A little background first: we had just merged two teams."
✓ Self-check
MISSION 2 · 📖 COMPRESS IT

🗜️ Kill the ramble

Your status update is rambling: "So first we had the meeting, and then Carlos said the API was down, and then we called support, and then…" Your manager's eyes are glazing over.
Say it out loud: compress the whole ramble into two sentences using "To cut a long story short,".
🔊 Model answer
"The API went down on launch morning. To cut a long story short, we shipped in three weeks."
✓ Self-check
💡 Compression is a senior skill. Juniors explain everything; seniors land the result.
MISSION 3 · 🎯 THE 90-SECOND STORY

🏆 Tell your story

"Tell me about a time you overcame a professional challenge." You have 90 seconds. Use chunks from all three moments 🎬 📖 🎯.
Say it out loud: set the scene, build with a turning point, land the takeaway. Time yourself.
🔊 Model answer (listen, then make it yours)
"So there we were, in a room with the board. It all started when the system crashed on launch day. And that's when everything changed — we stopped blaming and started shipping. In the end, we doubled our revenue. Looking back, the crisis was the best thing that happened to us."
✓ Self-check
