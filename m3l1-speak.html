🎙️ Your turn — the 60-second pitch

Record yourself (phone voice memo is fine). Then compare with the model. Tone is the grade.

Use chunks from at least 3 different groups (A · B · C · D):

1

🎯 Open your stance → kickoff meeting

The team is waiting. Open with a Group A chunk and plant your flag on the plan: From my perspective, / The way I see it, / If you ask me, __. No “in my opinion” — that’s the one-volume trap.

“From my perspective, the timeline is the real risk.”🔊 Model answer — tap to compare
2

🎚️ Push back on your boss → without breaking the relationship

Your boss loves the current plan. You don’t. Use a Group B softener — turn the volume DOWN: I’m inclined to believe __. Never the hard chunk upward (✗ “As far as I’m concerned” to your boss = insubordination).

“I’m inclined to believe the client will accept.”🔊 Model answer — tap to compare
3

🔨 Your 60-second pitch → steer it, then land it

Full arc in 60 seconds: open your stance (A), steer with a question (C), land the takeaway (D). Say all three out loud, then listen back. Model kit below — one chunk per moment:

“The way I see it, we should pilot this first.”🔊 A · open
“Have you considered a phased rollout?”🔊 C · steer
“The bottom line is, we need a decision today.”🔊 D · land — falling intonation!
